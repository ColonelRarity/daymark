# Approximate recipe nutrition

Anses. 2025. Ciqual French food composition table. doi:10.5281/zenodo.17550133
Source workbook: Table Ciqual 2025_ENG_2025_11_03.xlsx. kcal column10, protein14, carbohydrate16, fat17 (zero-based). Actual reference food codes/names and per100g data are in dist/nutrition-data.js. Missing trace macros treated as0; never claim laboratory precision. Energy calculated independently from reported kcal rather than macro4/4/9.

Recipe ingredient amounts are per one base recipe portion. Grains/meat raw; prepared legumes/canned food drained. Egg50g edible part; ml liquids approximated1g. Curds use an explicit assumed5% label121kcal, P17 F5 C1.8; not a Ciqual measurement. Generic substitutions: lavash=pita; sour cream=light thick cream; mince=chicken breast; berries=raspberry; frozen vegetables=unseasoned mixed couscous vegetables. Product brand/fat/oil differences mean estimates vary. The source modal exposes these assumptions. User can edit diary estimates; image AI remains a demonstration, no live AI inference.
